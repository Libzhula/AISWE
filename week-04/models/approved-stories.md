# Approved stories — Smart Campus study room booking

**Source of this set:** my week-03 stories, revised

## Scenario

Students view room availability, book a room, and cancel their own bookings. Administrators block or unblock rooms and review usage.

- **R1** Future start, with duration greater than 0 and at most 2 hours.
- **R2** Active bookings for the same room cannot overlap.
- **R3** A blocked room cannot accept a new booking.
- **R4** A successful booking produces a confirmation.

## Approved Week 03 stories

# User stories — Smart Campus study room booking

### US-01
**Story:** As a Student, I want to view study room availability, so that I can choose a free room and time before making a booking.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** Availability is shown only for library study rooms managed by the campus system.

### US-02
**Story:** As a Student, I want to book an available study room for a future time slot, so that I can reserve a place for individual or group study.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** A valid booking request includes a room, start time, end time, and student identity from the campus web application.

### US-03
**Story:** As a Student, I want to cancel my own booking, so that the room becomes available for other students.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** A student can cancel only a booking that they previously made.

### US-04
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to block or unblock a study room, so that unavailable rooms are not offered for new bookings.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** Blocking a room affects new booking attempts but does not automatically delete existing bookings.

### US-05
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to review study room usage over a selected period, so that library staff can understand demand and room utilization.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Usage review is based on booking records already held by the system.

### US-06
**Story:** As a Student, I want to receive confirmation when I book a room, so that I know the reservation was accepted.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Confirmation is generated by the system after a successful booking.

### US-07
**Story:** As a Student, I want to receive confirmation when I cancel a booking, so that I know the room was released.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Cancellation confirmation is generated only after a valid existing booking is cancelled.

**Out of scope**: payments, equipment in rooms, recurring bookings, waiting lists, notifications other than the booking confirmation, user registration.
